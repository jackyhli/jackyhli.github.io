01 — Research · Summer 2024

Self-calibrating interference suppression

An Arduino feedback loop that automatically calibrates a 6-bit RF attenuator to suppress interference in wireless receivers, using batch gradient descent for linear calibration and live detector feedback. Built with Emily Zhang in Prof. Marvin Onabajo's lab through Northeastern's Young Scholars Program, and presented to faculty and researchers at the program's final symposium.
